Engineering mindset

Design for failure. Automate the recovery path.

I approach infrastructure as a lifecycle: secure design, repeatable implementation, monitoring, controlled failure testing, recovery, and continuous improvement.

  1. 01

    Secure design

    Identity, access control, isolation, and safe defaults.

  2. 02

    Repeatable infrastructure

    Consistent deployment patterns and operational workflows.

  3. 03

    Observe and test

    Logs, metrics, alerts, and controlled failure scenarios.

  4. 04

    Recover and improve

    Validate recovery paths and turn incidents into stronger systems.
